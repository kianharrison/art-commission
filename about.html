Kian Harrison

Kian Harrison, known online as KianLooksBetter, is a digital artist, 2D/3D animator, motion graphics designer, and software engineering student in FET. His work blends expressive character illustration with clean visual storytelling, giving clients artwork and animation that feels polished, readable, and full of personality.

Across portraits, character art, animated story-time projects, short comedic pieces, and brand-focused motion graphics, Kian approaches each commission with careful planning, strong references, and attention to the details that make an idea feel complete. His software engineering background also shapes the way he works: organized, problem-solving focused, and precise about building a smooth experience from first brief to final delivery.
